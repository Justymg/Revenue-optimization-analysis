# Revenue-optimization-analysis
NOVATECH ELECTRONICS REVENUE OPTIMIZATION ANALYSIS


#1 PROJECT BACKGROUND
NovaTech Electronics aimed to analyze product performance, regional sales trends, and sales representative contribution to support revenue optimization and inventory planning decisions. 

Insights and recommendation areas
Product level Performance: dependency on few products, premium vs budget product trends
Sales Representative Performance: training opportunities, incentive recommendations
Monthly Revenue Comparisons: inventory planning, promotional timing


#2 DATA STRUCTURE & PREPARATIONS


The dataset was pre-cleaned; validation checks were performed before analysis.
Year : 2021
Rows : 1560





#3 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Total Revenue: 8M
Units Sold: 20K
Top Revenue Product: Dell XPS 13


Product analysis: 

The scatter plot illustrates a clear segmentation in product performance: 
Dell XPS 13 generated disproportionately high revenue, indicating strong demand for premium laptops.
OnePlus Buds sold high volumes but contributed relatively lower revenue, suggesting lower-ticket but fast-moving inventory. 
The Dell XPS13 serves as a high-value, lower-volume revenue driver, whereas the OnePlus Buds demonstrate a high-volume, lower-revenue profile.
Bubble size denotes unit price, highlighting trade-off between sales volume and average selling price.



Revenue trends remained relatively stable across product categories, with noticeable spikes during quarter-opening months and a significant increase in December sales. 


Sales Representative Analysis:


Several representatives performed strongly during the first half of the year but struggled to maintain consistency later, while top performers showed gradual revenue growth and stronger year-end performance. 
High-performing representatives displayed stronger consistency and improved momentum during later quarters.
Several representatives experienced sharp mid-year declines, suggesting possible gaps in sales adaptability or product specialization.
Laptop-focused sales strategies appeared to contribute disproportionately to higher overall revenue generation.







Monthly Trend Analysis:



Revenue displayed recurring quarter-opening revenue spikes, particularly during April, July, October, and December.
December recorded the highest monthly revenue, suggesting strong year-end purchasing activity.
Mid-quarter periods showed comparatively weaker performance, indicating possible seasonality in customer demand.
Revenue fluctuations highlight opportunities for strategic promotional planning and inventory optimization.

#4 RECOMMENDATIONS
Product 💻: 
Increase inventory allocation for high-performing laptop models
Location 🌆: 
Launch promotions in weaker cities like Cochin to increase regional sales contribution.
Sales Rep 👔: 
Replicate high-performing sales approaches across teams through targeted coaching and product-focused sales training. 
Revenue 📈:
Focus marketing spend during Q4 seasonality peaks
